# Invoices and tax receipts

<!-- doc_id: billing-invoices-01 -->
<!-- category: billing -->

Invoices are emailed to billing contacts within 24 hours of each charge. PDF copies live under Settings → Billing → Invoices. VAT/GST IDs can be set on the same page and appear on the next invoice. Failed payments retry for 7 days before the workspace is moved to read-only mode.
